# Impact factors

**Read this before quoting any number in the `Impact factor` column.**

The selection rule for this collection was a Journal Impact Factor of **5 or above**. Every
one of the ten journals used clears that bar comfortably on every source consulted, so the
threshold decision is safe. The *precise* values are less safe, and are recorded here with
their provenance so you can replace them with figures read directly from Journal Citation
Reports before anything goes into a manuscript.

## What was and was not done

These values were **not** read from Clarivate's Journal Citation Reports, which is
subscription-only. They were compiled from publicly reported figures for the 2023 JCR year.
Public aggregator sites disagree with each other, sometimes substantially, because they
variously report:

- the JCR **Journal Impact Factor** (the metric the threshold refers to),
- Scopus **CiteScore**, which is a different metric on a different citation window,
- a different JCR year than the one labelled, or
- a projected or "predicted" value.

Where sources conflicted, the publisher's own statement was preferred.

## Journals excluded on the threshold

These were topically closer to ovarian cancer or to older adults than some of the records
that were included, and were rejected only because of the JIF ≥ 5 rule. If the threshold is
ever relaxed, start here.

| Journal | Approx. JIF | What was lost |
|---|---|---|
| The Oncologist | ~4.8 | Wilson 2018, HRQOL reporting in **ovarian cancer** phase III trials — the closest miss in the whole exercise |
| Nutrients | ~4.8 | Rinninella 2019, nutritional interventions in **ovarian cancer** RCTs |
| Gynecologic Oncology | ~4.5 | Several ovarian-specific treatment and patient-values reviews |
| International Journal of Gynecological Cancer | ~4.1 | Kumar 2019, quality of life after surgery for advanced **ovarian cancer** |
| Epidemiologic Reviews | ~3.4–4.1 | Atkinson 2017, patient-reported physical function measures in cancer trials |
| Journal of Cancer Survivorship | ~3.4 | Ahmed-Lecheheb 2016, **ovarian cancer** survivors' quality of life |
| Psycho-Oncology | ~3.3 | Psychosocial interventions in gynaecological cancer |
| Journal of Geriatric Oncology | ~3.0 | Martin 2020, functional recovery after gynaecological cancer surgery in **older women** |
| Supportive Care in Cancer | ~2.8 | Wells 2018, effect of patient navigation on **satisfaction** with cancer care |
| BMJ Open | ~2.4 | Watts 2015, depression and anxiety in **ovarian cancer** |

The pattern is worth noting: the ovarian-specific and geriatric-oncology literature sits
almost entirely in the 2.4–4.8 band. A JIF ≥ 5 rule is, in practice, a rule that excludes
ovarian cancer specificity — which is why six of the eight categories had to broaden the
cancer type.
