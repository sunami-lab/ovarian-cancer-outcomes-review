### P_ovarian - Population - ovarian cancer
#
# Billson 2013 (sheet 5, Ovid lines 26-29) and Elattar 2011 (sheet 1, lines 1-7), translated
# to PubMed syntax. The fallopian tube and primary peritoneal terms are an addition: current
# WHO classification treats high-grade serous carcinoma of ovary, fallopian tube and
# peritoneum as one entity, and trials from about 2010 onward recruit and report them

#
